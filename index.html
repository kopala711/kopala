About Kopala

Kopala is a dating app for singles, bringing families together.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy explains what Kopala ("we") collects, why, who can see it, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password, or your Google or Apple sign-in (we receive your name and email from them).
  • Profile: username, gender, birthday (to confirm you are 18 or older; only your age is shown), city (never shown to other members), heritage, Melet villages, photos, your answer to a profile question, optional details (education, work, height, smoking, drinking, children, languages), and, if you choose, your Instagram handle.
  • Activity: likes, passes, matches, messages, blocks and reports.
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can tell you about matches and messages.
  • Support messages: what you write to us through Contact support in the app, with your username and email so we can reply.

We do not collect your phone number, your location, your contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To create your account, confirm your email, and show your profile to members you could match with.
  • To deliver messages between matched members and send notifications.
  • To keep members safe: reviewing new profiles and reports, filtering harmful messages, and removing rule-breakers.
  • To answer your support requests.

Who can see your information

  • Other members see your profile (not your city, birthday or email). Your Instagram handle is shown only to people you match with. Only matches can message you.
  • The Kopala admin team sees new profiles for approval and the reported content needed to review a report.
  • Service providers that run Kopala for us, only for that purpose: Supabase (database, photo storage and sign-in, hosted in the United States), Google (sending the email codes, Google sign-in, and Android notification delivery), Expo (notification delivery) and Cloudflare (the "I'm not a robot" check at sign-up and sign-in).
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your information stays while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, likes, matches and messages. Reports about safety problems, including the messages attached to them, are kept for up to one year to protect members, then deleted.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile anytime in the app.
  • Block or report anyone from their profile or your chat.
  • Delete your account anytime (see Delete your account).
  • Ask for a copy of your information or for any correction by emailing us (see Support).

Security

Your information is protected by sign-in and by database security rules that let each member read only what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Age

Kopala is only for adults 18 and older. We delete any account we learn belongs to someone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and tell you in the app.

Child safety standards

Kopala is for adults only and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

  • Every member must confirm they are 18 or older, and the app checks the birthday at sign-up.
  • Every new profile is reviewed by our team before it becomes visible.
  • Members can report anyone who seems to be under 18 from any profile or chat; these reports are reviewed first.
  • Accounts involved in child sexual abuse or exploitation, or belonging to anyone under 18, are removed immediately.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and cooperate with law enforcement as required by law.

To report a child-safety concern, use Report in the app or email us (see Support) with the subject Child safety.
